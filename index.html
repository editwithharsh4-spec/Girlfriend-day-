Tumhari care meri sabse badi taqat hai. Tumhari choti choti baatein, tumhara gussa, tumhara pyar, tumhara har andaaz mujhe aur zyada tumse pyar karna sikha deta hai. Tumhare saath bitaya hua har ek pal meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat yaad hai. Meri Bahu Bacha ❤️ main sirf aaj nahi balki har din tumhe isi tarah pyar karna chahta hu. Main sach me tumse shaadi karna chahta hu aur har janam me tumhara hi saath chahta hu. Main wada karta hu ki zindagi me chahe kitni bhi mushkilein aayein, main hamesha tumhara haath pakadkar chalunga. Tumhari khushi meri zimmedari rahegi aur tumhari muskaan meri sabse badi jeet hogi. Thank You meri life me aane ke liye. Thank You mujhe itna pyar dene ke liye. Thank You meri duniya ko itna khoobsurat banane ke liye.
